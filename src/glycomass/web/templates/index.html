01 · Calculators

Three engines. One chemistry.

Each calculator shares a single, validated mass model — amino-acid and monosaccharide compositions resolved through the same isotope engine that draws your spectrum.

/peptide

Glycopeptide

Peptide sequence plus Hex / HexNAc / Fuc / Sia, with carbamidomethylation and deamidation. Returns m/z, formula and isotope spectrum at any charge.

Calculate →
/protein

Glycoprotein

Full-length sequences with disulfide-bridge corrections and selectable instrument resolution — denatured or natively folded.

Calculate →
/glycan

Free glycan

Released glycans with permethylation, peracetylation, reduced ends, 2-AB / 2-AA / procainamide labels, custom elemental modifications — plus sodium-adduct handling for positive mode.
