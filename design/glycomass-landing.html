01 · Calculators

Three engines. One chemistry.

Each calculator shares a single, validated mass model — amino-acid and monosaccharide compositions resolved through the same isotope engine that draws your spectrum.

/peptide

Glycopeptide

Peptide sequence plus Hex / HexNAc / Fuc / Sia, with carbamidomethylation and deamidation. Returns m/z, formula and isotope spectrum at any charge.

Calculate →
/protein

Glycoprotein

Full-length sequences with disulfide-bridge corrections and selectable instrument resolution — denatured or natively folded.

Free glycan

02 · Identifier

Strip the glycan, find the peptide.

Upload an MGF file and the identifier classifies glycopeptide spectra by their oxonium ions, infers the peptide-backbone mass from HexNAc fragment ladders, and rewrites each precursor to its deglycosylated mass — ready for a standard search.

  • ✓ Oxonium-ion classification (HexNAc, HexNAc+Hex)
  • ✓ Peptide mass from the Pep+HexNAc fragment ladder
  • ✓ Cleaned MGF export, processed in the background
